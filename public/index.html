Invite → Calendar

Paste or forward an invite, we put it on your calendar. Never miss an event again.

How to use — tap to expand/collapse
  1. Get the invite — copy the invitation text, or save the poster/image from WhatsApp, Telegram, email, etc.
  2. Paste the text into the box below, and / or attach the invitation image.
  3. Press Parse invite — the app reads it and fills in the event details for you.
  4. Review the details (title, date, time, place). Fix anything that looks wrong.
  5. Add it to your calendar:
    • Google Calendar — press “Add to Google Calendar”. First time you’ll be asked to sign in to Google.
    • Apple Calendar — press “Copy event for Apple Calendar”, then run your iOS Shortcut (which reads the copied event and saves it on your iPhone).

Tip: you can paste a plain invite as text, or attach a full poster image — both work, depending on the AI model in use.
